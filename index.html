About MS Dhoni

Captain Cool • Legend • Inspiration

Mahendra Singh Dhoni

Mahendra Singh Dhoni is one of the greatest captains in the history of cricket. He led India to victory in the T20 World Cup 2007, ICC Cricket World Cup 2011, and Champions Trophy 2013.

Known for his calm mindset, match finishing ability, and helicopter shot, Dhoni has inspired millions of cricket fans across the world.
